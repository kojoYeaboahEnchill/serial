Serial Dilution & Plate Count

Ten-fold dilutions of an E. coli broth (1 mL into 9 mL saline), spread 0.1 mL of 10−4 to 10−7 in duplicate, incubate, count plates with 30–300 colonies and calculate CFU/mL.

    Result

    CFU/mL = colonies ÷ (0.1 mL × dilution). Average the countable plates (30–300).
